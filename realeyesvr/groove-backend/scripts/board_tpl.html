V1-A carries over. It was two shots; it is now one 13.941 s tracking shot sampled at two panels, A/1 and A/2 — the structure your 180_degree board uses. That matters beyond the drawing: one shot with N panels is exactly one aleph2 call with N timed keyframes, so the film-grammar unit and the API unit are the same thing. Cuts happen between calls, in ffmpeg, where they are free and frame-exact.

Watch the direction check on this one. In a following shot the subject holds position while the background slides the other way, so the frame-motion measurement in direction.py will read leftward for a man walking right and mirror a good clip. Tracking shots need to skip that check.
