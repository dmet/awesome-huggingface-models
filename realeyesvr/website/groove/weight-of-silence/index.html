    Slice 3 · Render · cut

    Pay only for the shots that move.

    Each approved panel becomes the first frame of its video, so the Runway shots match the board. Every request is checked against a credit limit, saved the moment it's sent, and never bought twice. Four shots, 150 credits, exactly as quoted. Stills and the edit are made locally for free.

    What we learned

    The first renders were living photos, so we tested the prompt.

    Shot 29, the star leaving her hands. The first prompt described the picture, which Runway already has. The fix was a prompt that only says what moves. Same panel, same 6 seconds:

    The glass jar slips from her fingers and tumbles away below her, spilling light. She arches back and spreads her arms wide, hair and cardigan lifting and rippling as if underwater. Overhead the star swells and flares. The camera slowly tilts up, following her gaze.

    Next: every Runway shot gets its own motion line. Gen-4 Turbo stays the default, and Gen-4.5 becomes a per-shot upgrade for the big moments.
